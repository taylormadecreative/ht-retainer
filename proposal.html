Why a term

The dates are set months out. Everything around them moves.

Convocation, Homecoming, giving season: those are fixed. What moves is everything around them, the travel, the timing, and the room you happen to be standing in when the message is due.

So the work happens on the other side of it. You record whenever the week allows, in whatever conditions you have, and it is finished here to the standard your office puts its name on.

That is what a term buys. Not more of your hours, but a standing way to get your words out on a set week, to the churches, the campus and the partner schools.

How one gets made

Two ways, and you pick per message.

Send us anything.

Shot on your phone, in a hotel, in the car, at the end of a long day. No crew, no light, no time to get ready. Send it exactly like that and it comes back looking deliberate.

  • Relit and color corrected, so the room stops mattering
  • Audio cleaned and leveled to broadcast
  • Captions in maroon and gold, sized for a thumb
  • Vertical for social, wide for screens and email

Or send us nothing.

For the weeks with no window at all, your approved likeness carries it. Built from the studio session you already sat for in August, so it is your face, your hair, your suit.

  • You approve a script, the finished video comes back that week
  • Licensed to your office alone, revocable in writing
  • Nothing renders without your written approval on the script
  • The authorization, already drafted for your counsel

September to November

Why a term, and not one video.

Because this is what arrives between now and December whether or not there is a production conversation in front of it.

September

Welcome Week and Convocation

A new class just arrived and their parents just drove home. Your welcome, a campus carousel, one email to families.

October

Homecoming

The alumni moment of the year, and the one with real giving attached. A countdown, your invitation, coverage on the day, a thank you the week after.

October 31

The costumed film you asked for first

An original character, written for you, not borrowed from anyone. It has to start by the last week of September to be finished, approved and posted before the thirty first.

November

Giving season

Where a president on camera moves actual money. A giving day push, a thank you to donors, a message that can go to every church and board member on your list.

Every week

The invitations

Churches, banquets, ceremonies, partner schools, city functions. The part that never stops, and the reason this is a term.
