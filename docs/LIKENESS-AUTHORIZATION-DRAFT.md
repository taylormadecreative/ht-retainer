# Presidential Likeness Authorization

**Draft for review by counsel. Not executed.**

Between **Huston-Tillotson University**, Office of the President ("the University")
and **Taylormade Creative** ("the Studio").

Concerning the likeness, voice and image of **Dr. Melva K. Wallace**, 7th President and CEO
("the President"), as captured in the studio session of August 8, 2026.

---

## 1. What is authorized

The Studio may create and render a digital likeness of the President, built only from footage and
stills the President sat for, for the sole purpose of producing communications issued by the Office
of the President.

## 2. Script approval, every time

No likeness is rendered without a written script approved in advance by the Office of the President.
Approval of one script is not approval of a pattern, a series, or a future use.

## 3. Exclusive to this office

The likeness is licensed exclusively to the Office of the President for the term of the engagement.
The Studio will not use it, or any part of it, for any other client, for its own promotion, for a
portfolio, in a proposal, or in any public showcase, without separate written permission naming the
specific use.

## 4. Prohibited uses, without exception

The likeness will not be used for:

- Political endorsement, campaign, or advocacy of any candidate, party or ballot measure
- Any third party, sponsor, donor or commercial partner
- Fundraising solicitation that misstates the University's position
- News, crisis communication, emergency notice, or any statement of fact a reader would reasonably
  need to trust as live and current
- Any statement the President has not personally approved in writing

## 5. No training, no derivative models

Nothing derived from the President's likeness, voice or footage will be used to train, fine-tune,
improve or evaluate any model or system. No copy of the source material is retained beyond what is
required to produce approved work.

## 6. Disclosure

The Studio's recommendation, which the University may accept or overrule in writing: any piece
published outside the University's own channels carries a short production note. Internal greetings
and messages to invited functions do not require one.

## 7. Revocation and deletion

The University may revoke this authorization at any time, in writing, for any reason and with no
cause required. On revocation the Studio will, within five business days:

- Stop all production using the likeness
- Delete the rendered likeness and all derived files
- Confirm deletion in writing

Work already approved and published before revocation may remain published at the University's
discretion.

## 8. Ownership

Finished deliverables and their source files belong to the University on payment. The rendered
likeness itself remains licensed rather than transferred, so that the protections in sections 2
through 7 continue to govern its use for as long as it exists.

## 9. Term

This authorization runs with the engagement and ends when the engagement ends, unless revoked
sooner under section 7.
