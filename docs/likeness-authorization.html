Presidential Likeness Authorization

Draft for review by counsel · not executed

Between Huston-Tillotson University, Office of the President (the University) and Taylormade Creative (the Studio), concerning the likeness, voice and image of Dr. Melva K. Wallace, 7th President and CEO (the President), as captured in the studio session of August 8, 2026.

OneWhat is authorized

The Studio may create and render a digital likeness of the President, built only from footage and stills the President sat for, for the sole purpose of producing communications issued by the Office of the President.

TwoScript approval, every time

No likeness is rendered without a written script approved in advance by the Office of the President. Approval of one script is not approval of a pattern, a series, or any future use.

ThreeExclusive to this office

The likeness is licensed exclusively to the Office of the President for the term of the engagement. The Studio will not use it, or any part of it, for another client, for its own promotion, in a portfolio, in a proposal, or in any public showcase, without separate written permission naming the specific use.

FourProhibited uses, without exception

  • Political endorsement, campaign, or advocacy of any candidate, party or ballot measure
  • Any third party, sponsor, donor or commercial partner
  • Fundraising solicitation that misstates the University's position
  • News, crisis communication, emergency notice, or any statement of fact a reader would reasonably need to trust as live and current
  • Any statement the President has not personally approved in writing

FiveNo training, no derivative models

Nothing derived from the President's likeness, voice or footage will be used to train, fine-tune, improve or evaluate any model or system. No copy of the source material is retained beyond what is required to produce approved work.

SixDisclosure

The Studio's recommendation, which the University may accept or overrule in writing: any piece published outside the University's own channels carries a short production note. Internal greetings and messages to invited functions do not require one.

SevenRevocation and deletion

The University may revoke this authorization at any time, in writing, for any reason, with no cause required. On revocation the Studio will, within five business days, stop all production using the likeness, delete the rendered likeness and all derived files, and confirm the deletion in writing. Work already approved and published before revocation may remain published at the University's discretion.

EightOwnership

Finished deliverables and their source files belong to the University on payment. The rendered likeness itself remains licensed rather than transferred, so that the protections above continue to govern its use for as long as it exists.

NineTerm

This authorization runs with the engagement and ends when the engagement ends, unless revoked sooner under section seven.
