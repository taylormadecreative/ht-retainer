The question still on the table

“Would you like me to recreate everything according to the guidelines? Or anything specific like just the social media post?”

The answer, plainly: everything, on the new guidelines — and then again next month, and the month after that.

A brand identity system is not a one-time conversion. It is a standard that has to hold across every post, every video, every email and every printed program for the whole year, which means somebody has to be producing against it continuously rather than re-converting a back catalog once and hoping it holds.

So this document does not propose a project. It proposes a standing month: a fixed set of finished work produced for the Office of the President, on your calendar, in your identity system, and approved by your office before anything publishes.

The last two weeks were one month of it, run at speed. What follows is the same thing with a calendar behind it, and it would sit under Marketing.

The working method

Two lanes, so a bad travel week never costs you a post.

Some weeks there is a window to record and no window to get ready for it. Some weeks there is no window at all. The month has to hold either way, so there are two ways in and you pick per message, not per month.

01

You send us thirty seconds off your phone.

Hotel room, back seat, ten minutes between meetings. Bad light, no crew, no window to get ready. Send it exactly like that.

  • Color corrected and relit so the room stops mattering
  • Audio cleaned, room noise pulled out, levels broadcast-normal
  • Captions in HT gold on maroon, word by word, sized for a thumb
  • Cut to length, end card, wordmark, and your call to action
  • Delivered vertical for social and wide for screens and email
02

Or you send us nothing at all.

For the weeks where there is no window, your presidential double carries the message. Built from your own studio session — your face, your hair, your suit.

  • You approve a script; a finished video comes back the same week
  • Thirty seconds by design — the format short-form actually rewards
  • Greetings, welcomes, congratulations, invitations
  • One recording, addressed to many rooms in a single weekend
  • Licensed exclusively to your office, revocable in writing at any time

Why thirty seconds

You named it yourself on August 10: maybe I need to use it for shorter scripts. The cut you watched ran 3:25, which is long for one unbroken shot in any form, filmed or otherwise. Thirty seconds is the standard now, on both lanes.

On the reference you sent August 16

That approach follows a real performance frame by frame, so it needs you on camera to exist at all — which is the part of the week you do not have. Lane 01 is how you get that same living quality out of thirty seconds shot on a phone. Lane 02 covers the weeks when even that is too much.

To start on the term

Six things,
none of them long.

Each one sits with your two offices, and most are a single reply. The first three are all we need to start building September.

  1. Which way in

    The Fall Term block, or a standing month and which of the three. That single answer sets everything else in motion.

  2. The master event calendar

    Whatever your Marketing office already keeps. It becomes the production schedule, and every deliverable gets dated backward from it. It also settles the dates left open in the fall calendar above.

  3. Brand vectors, and the student destination

    SVG or EPS files for the marks and patterns, and written confirmation of the URL you want students sent to for account and enrollment questions. Both from Marketing, both quick.

  4. The likeness authorization, signed

    One page, drafted and ready for your counsel to read. It gets signed before the first render of the double, every time, without being asked for.

  5. Your paperwork and your thresholds

    The vendor agreement your office uses, your competitive-bid threshold, and your fiscal year start. Those three numbers decide how the term is written, and we would rather fit your process than test it.

  6. Where the numbers stand today

    Current reach and engagement on the channels this touches, and whatever you already track on enrollment yield or giving participation. Not to promise against — to report against, so by month two there is a real figure rather than an impression.
