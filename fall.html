September

Fall in love.

September asks for nothing. It lets Austin, parents, and alumni fall in love first. Every piece is pride, energy, and faces, anchored by Convocation.

October

Belong.

October turns attention into belonging. Charter Day anchors the month while FAFSA, advising, and registration carry the practical work underneath.

November + December

Invest.

By November the audience has watched the story all fall. Now, and only now, it is asked to invest in it: gratitude first, Giving Tuesday, then the year-end close.

September and October never ask for money. That is what lets November and December ask twice.

Before this is final

Two answers I need from you.

1

When is Homecoming?

It is not on the academic calendar, and the answer changes how October and the spring get built. If it lives in February, the fall map holds; if a fall date exists, it becomes an anchor.

2

Which number leads the season: 150 years since 1875, or the 152nd Charter?

Both are true. The season should say one of them loudly and consistently, everywhere, so the story compounds instead of splitting.
